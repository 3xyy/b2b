We're excited to announce the **Tech to Treasure Hackathon** — an environmental
hackathon powered by Bin to Better, where builders turn ideas into impact in just
8 hours.

## The details

- **When:** March 28, 2026, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- **Where:** Tully Library, 880 Tully Rd, San Jose, CA 95111
- **Prizes:** $4,000+ in credits for first place, plus awards down to fifth and a
  special Wolfram Award.

From AI agents to data-driven platforms, participants will work together to tackle
sustainability challenges using technology — with hands-on mentorship and workshops
led by industry engineers.

## Why we're doing it

Environmental innovation shouldn't be limited to experts. Our goal is to give
builders the tools, mentorship, and space to turn ideas into working prototypes —
and to grow a community of creators making lasting impact together.
